Why choose this app?

Built to help ourselves on our own learning journey, we are happy to share the results with everyone.

✈️ 100% Offline: mobile and e-reader friendly

Download courses and learn on the tube, on a plane, or out in the wild. No internet connection or custom software required.

🔒 Private and secure

All your learning data is stored locally on your device, not on our servers. There is no risk of password or email leaks, and we will never sell your information to third parties.

💸 Free and accessible

We believe access to essential learning tools should be free and open to everyone. The core learning experience is completely free, with no ads or hidden paywalls.
