Why choose this app?

Built to help ourselves on our own learning journey, we are happy to share the results with everyone.

✈️ 100% Offline: mobile and e-reader friendly

Download courses and learn on the tube, on a plane, or out in the wild. Once course downloaded, no internet connection required.

🔒 Private and secure

All your learning data is stored locally on your device. There is no risk of credential leaks, and we will not sell your information to third parties.

💸 Free and accessible

We believe access to essential learning tools should be free and open to everyone. The core learning experience is completely free, with no ads or hidden paywalls.
